Sacred Agni Pheras

The Seven Sacred Vows

“With the sacred fire as our eternal witness, each step seals a promise of nourishment, strength, prosperity, and companionship for lifetimes to come.”
Step 1 • Pratham Kadam

Nourishment & Health

To share the responsibility of providing nourishment and welfare for our home with pure devotion.

Step 2 • Dwitiya Kadam

Strength & Courage

To stand by each other through joy and sorrow, giving strength and protecting one another unconditionally.

Step 3 • Tritiya Kadam

Prosperity & Wealth

To work diligently with honesty, growing in spiritual, emotional, and material abundance together.

Step 4 • Chaturtha Kadam

Harmony & Respect

To honor both our families, nurturing wisdom, mutual respect, and peace across generations.

Step 5 • Panchama Kadam

Compassion & Dharma

To walk the path of righteousness, truth, and mutual care for all living beings.

Step 6 • Shashta Kadam

Longevity & Seasons

To walk together in harmony across all seasons of life, blessed with health, vigor, and peace.

Step 7 • Saptama Kadam

Eternal Companionship

To become eternal best friends, soulmates united in mind and spirit throughout this lifetime and beyond.
